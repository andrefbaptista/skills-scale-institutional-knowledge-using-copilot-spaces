# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to refine acceptance criteria and business value
- Coordinate with Project Managers on scope, schedule, and dependencies
- Work with QA specialists to validate defect fixes and release readiness
- Collaborate with Technical Leads on design trade-offs and technical standards

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Align with Project Managers on milestones, capacity, and sequencing
- Partner with Developers on feasibility, trade-offs, and release scope
- Work with Business Sponsors to confirm strategic alignment and priority
- Coordinate with Customer Success and Support to understand user feedback and adoption issues

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Connect Product Managers, Developers, and stakeholders through a shared delivery rhythm
- Support Technical Leads and QA in tracking dependencies and release readiness
- Escalate blocking risks to Business Sponsors or Product Leads when needed
- Coordinate with DevOps and Release Engineers for deployment readiness and operational planning

---

## QA / Testing Specialists

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Specialists ensure product quality by validating requirements, running test plans, and confirming that features meet acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute test plans for features and bug fixes
- Validate functionality against acceptance criteria and user workflows
- Identify, document, and help triage defects with clear reproduction steps
- Coordinate manual and automated testing coverage with engineering teams
- Verify release readiness in staging and pre-production environments

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce business risk
- Improve quality consistency across releases
- Build confidence in production readiness and user experience

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions with Developers and Product Managers
- Defect triage and release readiness reviews
- Quality and risk updates during sprint or milestone reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborates closely with Developers on defect investigation and validation
- Works with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are testable and measurable
- Provides release signal to Project Managers and Business Sponsors before go-live
- Coordinates with Security and DevOps teams on staging checks and operational validation

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide design direction, technical oversight, and mentorship to keep delivery aligned with engineering standards and system goals.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture decisions and technical trade-offs
- Review designs and implementation approaches for scalability and maintainability
- Mentor developers and help resolve complex technical blockers
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and opportunities for simplification
- Help maintain architecture documentation and engineering standards

### Goals
- Reduce technical debt and delivery risk
- Keep systems aligned with long-term quality and maintainability goals
- Support team velocity without sacrificing quality or reliability

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Risk and dependency assessments during planning
- Code review and mentoring conversations

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Aligns with Product and Project Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and delivery constraints
- Supports Developers in design decisions and technical execution
- Helps QA understand critical paths, edge cases, and integration risk
- Coordinates with Security and DevOps on platform, compliance, and deployment implications

---

## Scrum Masters / Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Leads help the team operate effectively by facilitating flow, removing blockers, and keeping the delivery process healthy and predictable.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies and delivery rituals
- Help the team identify and remove blockers, dependencies, and process friction
- Improve sprint health, flow, and visibility across the team
- Support estimation, planning, and retrospectives with a focus on continuous improvement
- Keep project activities aligned with agreed team working agreements

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and predictability
- Create a healthy, disciplined delivery cadence
- Reduce friction caused by unclear process or unresolved dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups, planning, and retrospective sessions
- Blocker tracking and team workflow discussions
- Delivery health updates to Project Managers and stakeholders

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Project Managers to keep schedules, risks, and dependencies visible
- Support Product Managers in backlog refinement and prioritization conversations
- Help Developers maintain focus and flow by surfacing impediments early
- Work with QA and Technical Leads to align delivery plans with testing and technical readiness

---

## Business Sponsors / Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors and Stakeholders provide strategic direction, funding, priority, and decision authority to ensure projects deliver clear business value.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives, desired outcomes, and priority for the initiative
- Review and approve scope, milestones, and high-level trade-offs
- Resolve organizational blockers and provide necessary resources
- Evaluate business impact and success metrics throughout the project lifecycle
- Support go/no-go decisions for key milestones and launches

### Goals
- Ensure work is aligned with strategic business goals
- Maximize measurable business value and stakeholder confidence
- Support timely decision-making and executive alignment

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and steering committee updates
- Priority and scope decisions with Product and Project Leaders
- Business outcomes and post-release impact reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Provide strategic input to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Review delivery progress and progress against goals with PM and Product leads
- Approve key trade-offs and investment decisions affecting scope or timeline
- Receive risk and readiness updates from Project Managers and QA teams before launch

---

## Security / Compliance Officers

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers help protect the organization by ensuring projects follow required standards, reduce risk, and maintain secure delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and privacy implications of project work
- Identify and track security risks, vulnerabilities, and compliance requirements
- Validate that processes align with policy, standards, and regulatory obligations
- Partner with engineering teams on secure design and remediation planning
- Support release and incident review activities where risk or compliance concerns arise

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across initiatives
- Ensure project work reflects policy and customer trust requirements
- Support secure, auditable delivery across the product lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, design assessments, and policy discussions
- Risk and remediation status with Project Managers and Technical Leads
- Release readiness checks for security-sensitive changes

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads to review architecture and implementation risk
- Supports QA by validating security-relevant acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with DevOps teams on secure deployment and environment controls
- Provides guidance and escalation path for high-risk issues affecting product or compliance

---

## DevOps / Release Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers enable reliable delivery by managing environments, automation, deployment pipelines, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain deployment pipelines, automation, and infrastructure-as-code practices
- Coordinate release timing, environment readiness, and rollback planning
- Monitor operational health and support deployment verification
- Improve release reliability through automation and observability
- Partner with teams to reduce deployment friction and minimize outages

### Goals
- Increase delivery speed without compromising reliability
- Improve deployment consistency and recovery readiness
- Support smooth production changes and operational visibility

### Typical Communication
- Release planning, readiness, and post-deploy review meetings
- Deployment pipeline and operational health updates
- Incident coordination and rollback discussions during release events

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Project Managers to schedule and verify release windows
- Collaborates with Developers and QA on environment confidence and smoke testing
- Supports Technical Leads with automation and platform design needs
- Coordinates with Security teams on secure deployment controls and operational safeguards

---

## Customer Success / Support Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Leads capture user feedback, track adoption issues, and help translate customer needs into product improvements and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor customer feedback, support trends, and service escalations
- Identify recurring issues that may indicate product gaps or process problems
- Share customer impact and usage insights with Product and Delivery teams
- Support launch readiness by ensuring support documentation and escalation paths are in place
- Help prioritize follow-up work based on user experience and operational signals

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption
- Reduce friction for users and internal support teams
- Turn customer signals into actionable product and process improvements

### Typical Communication
- Feedback reviews and issue triage with Product and support teams
- Status updates tied to launches, bug fixes, and adoption trends
- Stakeholder updates on customer impact and support load

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Shares product usage and support feedback with Product Managers and Developers
- Provides QA and Project Managers with insight into user-impacting issues
- Coordinates with Business Sponsors on customer satisfaction and strategic outcomes
- Supports incidents and escalation paths alongside Security, DevOps, and Project teams
